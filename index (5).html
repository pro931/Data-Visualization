    How it was built in Tableau

    Tableau has no built-in radar chart, so the shape is drawn with calculated fields that turn each month into X and Y coordinates on a circle, then plotted as a polygon.

    1. Month: DATENAME('month', [Order Date]) gives the 12 spokes.
    2. Distance from centre: SUM([Sales]), so higher sales sit further out.
    3. Angle: a running sum that splits the circle into 12 equal slices and starts at the top (+π/2).
    4. X and Y: distance × cos(angle) and distance × sin(angle), placed on Columns and Rows.
    5. Changed the mark type to Polygon, coloured by year, and fixed both axes to ±120,000 so the circle stays round.
    Angle = -RUNNING_SUM( 2*PI() / MIN({COUNTD([Month])}) ) + PI()/2 X = [Distance from Center] * COS([Angle]) Y = [Distance from Center] * SIN([Angle])
